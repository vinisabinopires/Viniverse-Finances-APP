Goal:
Add a proper Transfers feature so the user can move money between existing accounts without counting it as income or expense.

Examples:
- Cash Wallet → US Checking
- US Checking → Cash Wallet
- US Checking → Brazil Investments
- US Checking → Brazil Checking/Savings
- Brazil Checking/Savings → Brazil Investments
- Any account → any account

Important financial rule:
Transfers are NOT income.
Transfers are NOT expenses.
Transfers should affect account balances and net worth, but should not inflate income/expense reports, budgets, or spending summaries.

- transfers should not affect income totals
