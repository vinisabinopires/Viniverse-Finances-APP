Goal:
Add a deterministic Budget Coach / Spending Guardrails feature that helps the user understand budget risk, spending pace, fixed commitment pressure, and practical next actions without using AI or external services.

Important financial rules:
- Transfers are not expenses and must not affect budget coach spending calculations.
- Recurring income is income.
- Recurring expenses are fixed commitments, but should only count as actual spending if generated as transactions.
- Budget Coach should be advisory only. It should not modify transactions, budgets, recurring rules, transfers, or accounts automatically.

3. Budget risk cards
For each budget in the selected month and currency, calculate:

- monthly limit
- spent so far
- remaining
- percent used
- day of month progress percent
- expected spend by today based on linear pace
- projected month-end spend based on current pace
- projected over/under amount
- daily remaining allowance

Risk levels:
- Safe: percent used is reasonably below month progress
- Watch: spending is slightly ahead of pace
- Risk: projected to exceed budget
- Over: already over budget

Use clear labels:
- Safe
- Watch
- Risk
- Over

Do not overcomplicate. Deterministic math only.

4. Spending pace insight
Add a section:
"Spending Pace"

Show:
- total budgeted amount
- total spent against budgeted categories
- total remaining
- projected month-end spending
- number of categories at risk
- number of categories over budget

Add plain-language insights like:
- "Dining is ahead of pace."
- "Groceries has $X left, about $Y/day."
- "Shopping is already over budget."

No AI. Use deterministic templates.

5. Fixed commitment pressure
Use active recurring expense rules from the Subscription Center logic.

Show:
- estimated monthly fixed commitments in selected currency
- estimated recurring income in selected currency
- fixed commitments as percent of recurring income, if income exists
- remaining estimated flexible income after fixed commitments

Risk labels:
- Comfortable
- Tight
- Heavy
- Critical

Example:
- Comfortable: fixed commitments below 35% of recurring income
- Tight: 35% to 50%
- Heavy: 50% to 70%
- Critical: above 70%

If no recurring income exists, show:
"Add recurring income to calculate commitment pressure."
